Background

Experience

How a campus help desk turned into a healthcare IT practice.

I got my start in the trenches of campus IT — first building the IT Help Center at Moorpark College from the ground up, then delivering enterprise support for a 280,000-user institution at Cal State Northridge. Those years taught me something no certification covers: technology only works when the people using it trust it.

From there I moved into web development and design, running a digital platform that reached more than 210,000 subscribers and later building client-facing products at a digital studio. That combination — infrastructure on one side, product and design on the other — is what pulled me toward healthcare, an industry where both matter enormously and neither can be an afterthought.

Since 2020 I've been at HealthSpaces, growing from onsite engineer deploying clinical infrastructure at facilities across the country, to leading nationwide healthcare IT operations, to Product Owner & Partner — owning the product roadmap and working directly with medical practices on their technology strategy. I bring that full arc to every engagement: the engineer's eye for systems, the product owner's sense of priorities, and a decade of lessons learned inside real practices.

FAQ

Questions practices ask me

Straight answers to the questions I hear most often.

What does a healthcare IT consultant do for a medical practice?

I help practices plan, implement, and manage the technology they run on — from EMR/EHR migrations and PACS imaging systems to VOIP phone systems, cloud migration, network infrastructure, and AI tools. The goal is always the same: technology that supports patient care instead of getting in its way.

What size practices do you work with?

I work directly with medical practices across the United States. As an independent consultant, you work with me personally from the first call to the finished project — not a rotating team of account managers.

We’re switching EMR systems. How do you help?

EMR migrations are high-stakes: downtime costs revenue and data mistakes risk compliance. I run a structured migration process that minimizes downtime, protects patient data through the move, and makes sure your staff is comfortable on the new system from day one.

What AI tools can you help our practice adopt?

I cut through the AI hype and implement what actually works for practices: AI-powered phone systems, smart scheduling, clinical transcription, and admin automation — plus practical AI use policies so your team knows what is safe and appropriate to use with patient information.

Can you work with our existing IT staff or vendors?

Absolutely. I can serve as your dedicated technology partner or work alongside your in-house team — and I can manage vendor and third-party relationships on your behalf, so contract negotiations and escalations don’t land on your front desk.

Do you work with practices outside your local area?

Yes — I work with medical practices across the United States.

How much does it cost to work with you?

Every practice’s needs are different, so engagements are scoped individually. The best next step is a conversation — reach out through the contact form and tell me what you’re dealing with.
