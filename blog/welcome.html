Welcome: what this blog is for

October 2, 2026 · 2 min read

After years of keeping medical practices' systems running, migrating their EMRs, and untangling their phones, I've noticed something: the same questions come up everywhere. Different cities, different specialties, same headaches.

So I'm writing the answers down.

Who this is for

Practice owners, office managers, and anyone who's ended up being "the tech person" at a medical practice without asking for the job. If you've ever wondered whether your IT setup is fine or quietly falling apart, you're in the right place.

What we'll cover

  • EMR migrations and replacements, without the horror stories
  • Cybersecurity and HIPAA: what's actually required vs. what's fear-selling
  • Phones, networks, and cloud: the unglamorous stuff that runs your day
  • AI tools that genuinely save time, and the ones that don't
  • What technology really costs a practice, and where the money goes

No jargon for jargon's sake. No 2,000-word whitepapers. Just practical thinking you can act on. New post every week.

If you're not sure where your practice stands today, start with the 2-minute IT Health Check. It'll tell you more than most consultants will in a first call.
